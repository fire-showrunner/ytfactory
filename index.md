# Privacy Policy — YT Factory

YT Factory is a private automation tool operated by a single person for managing their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

**Data accessed.** With the owner's consent via Google OAuth, the app accesses only the owner's own YouTube channels: uploading videos, reading channel and video information, and reading YouTube Analytics reports. It may also read and write the owner's own Google Sheets used for planning.

**Use of data.** Data is used solely to publish and manage the owner's videos and to review their performance. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

**Storage.** Access tokens and data are stored locally on the owner's computer and are not transferred elsewhere.

**Revoking access.** Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
